Architectural Narrative

An elevated urban sanctuary sculpted for generations.

Spread across 14 sprawling acres in the prime Kokapet–Narsingi corridor, Ira Moonglade is an iconic residential landmark comprising 2,400 residences distributed across 7 majestic 40-storey towers.

Designed around an expansive vehicle-free podium deck, the development seamlessly isolates all parking across 2 basements, stilt, and 4 podiums—elevating lush landscaped open spaces, community recreation, and family life high above road level.

Every aperture has been engineered for optimal solar progression, allowing natural cross-ventilation and uninhibited panoramic views across Kokapet lake and the Hyderabad skyline.
