Project Overview

Peaceful, elevated living built to last for generations.

Spread across 14 open acres in the prime Kokapet–Narsingi area, Ira Moonglade is a landmark community of 2,400 homes across 7 modern 40-storey towers.

Built around a large vehicle-free podium deck, all parking is kept separate across 2 basements, a stilt floor, and 4 podium levels—keeping gardens, play areas, and daily family life peaceful and safe above road level.

Every window and balcony is placed to bring in natural sunlight and fresh air, offering clear, open views of Kokapet lake and the Hyderabad skyline.
