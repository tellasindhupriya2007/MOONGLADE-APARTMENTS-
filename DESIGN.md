## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the pinnacle of high-end residential architecture and experiential real estate. Designed for ultra-high-net-worth buyers, investors, and architectural connoisseurs seeking a landmark estate in Kokapet, Hyderabad, the visual language merges the monumental grace of classical proportions with the pristine discipline of modern minimalist architecture.

The interface evokes serenity, exclusivity, and sovereign permanence. To achieve this:
- **Architectural Editorial:** The aesthetic mirrors luxury property monographs, high-fashion broadsheets, and haute architectural journals.
- **Generous Canvas & Precision Hairlines:** Negative space functions as a structural material, framing content like museum exhibitions. Crisp, subtle dividing rules replace heavy cards or dropshadows.
- **Sovereign Color Discipline:** Regal deep purple and champagne gold act strictly as focal accents against pure alabaster, crisp ivory, and charcoal typography.
- **Strict Geometric Restraint:** Curves are tempered and intentional. No pill shapes, squishy surfaces, or playful radiuses are permitted.

## Colors

The color palette reflects natural stone, polished metallurgical trims, night skies, and bespoke masonry.

- **Primary (`#3B1D54`) — Royal Imperial Purple:** Represents the sovereign heritage and nocturnal elegance of Moonglade. Used sparingly for authoritative brand elements, exclusive badges, high-priority primary actions, and dramatic typography accents.
- **Secondary (`#C5A059`) — Champagne Sovereign Gold:** Symbolizes architectural refinement, metallic finishes, and curated craftsmanship. Applied to active states, key data accents, bespoke icons, micro-delimiters, and subtle borders.
- **Tertiary & Text (`#1A1A1A`) — Basalt Slate:** Replaces harsh digital black with deep, warm architectural charcoal. Used for primary typography, editorial section titles, and architectural floor plan schematics.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FDFCFA` to `#FFFFFF`) — Alabaster Ivory:** An ultra-clean, gallery-grade foundation that allows photography, floor plans, and elevation renders to breathe without visual tension.
- **Structural Lines (`#E6E3DD`) — Travertine Hairline:** A warm, neutral stone border tone used strictly at 1px thickness for grids, separators, and structural table framing.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy juxtaposes classical romance with rational technical precision.

- **Headings (Playfair Display):** Majestic, high-contrast serif typography reserved for editorial narrative, elevation titles, and section statements. Line heights remain tight and balanced to evoke print typography.
- **Body & Specs (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Crisp, geometric sans-serif balancing human warmth with structural clarity. Body weights lean lighter (`300` and `400`) to preserve an airy, luxury feeling.
- **Eyebrows & Technical Labels:** Styled using `label-caps` in strict uppercase with expanded tracking (`0.15em`). This provides immediate architectural context (e.g., "TOWER A — LEVEL 42", "PENTHOUSE RESIDENCE").

## Layout & Spacing

The layout adheres to a classical 12-column architectural grid with generous, deliberate whitespace. Empty space is never treated as unfilled voids, but as a deliberate design medium to emphasize scale and luxury.

- **Desktop (12 Columns):** 4rem (64px) outer margin, 2rem (32px) gutters, and a maximum content width of 1440px for editorial readability. Full-width architectural renders break out of the container to span 100vw.
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** 2.5rem (40px) outer margin, 1.5rem (24px) gutters. Content shifts to balanced dual-column cards and staggered text-image pairings.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** 1.25rem (20px) outer margins, 1rem (16px) gutters. Editorial hierarchies stack vertically with uninterrupted full-bleed image displays and hairline dividers.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Sections are delineated using `space-xl` (4.5rem / 72px) to 6rem gaps, ensuring quiet transitions between distinct building features, floor plans, and amenities.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system consciously rejects diffuse, heavy drop shadows and modern neumorphic blurs in favor of architectural purity and tactile flat surfaces.

- **Hairline Tiers:** Depth is established primarily through 1px Travertine hairlines (`#E6E3DD`) that demarcate zones, columns, and architectural specs.
- **Tonal Stepping:** Overlays and elevation changes use subtle solid background transitions: `#FDFCFA` (canvas), `#F7F5F0` (subtle inset/spec container), and `#FFFFFF` (elevated focal element).
- **Architectural Framing Shadow:** When popovers, lightbox galleries, or scheduling dialogs require floating elevation, employ an ultra-subtle, non-muddy ambient drop: `box-shadow: 0 16px 40px -12px rgba(59, 29, 84, 0.08)`. The slight royal purple tint warms the shadow, avoiding synthetic grey cast.
- **Subtle Glass Frost:** For sticky navigation headers only: background `#FDFCFA` at 92% opacity with a `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and a 1px bottom border in `#E6E3DD`.

## Shapes

The architectural shape language is completely sharp (`roundedness: 0`). 

- **Right-Angle Precision:** Buttons, cards, image containers, input fields, and tags feature pure 90-degree corners. This reinforces the structural rectilinearity of modern towers, clean stone slabs, and floor plan grids.
- **Anti-Pill Philosophy:** Rounded pill buttons and heavy rounded corners are strictly prohibited. Luxury is conveyed through proportion, alignment, and hairline framing rather than softness.
- **Architectural Accents:** Subtle notched corners (45-degree chamfers) or bespoke 1px metallic corner pins in Champagne Gold (`#C5A059`) may be utilized on premier modal panels and floor-plan interactive frames.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Sovereign:** Solid deep purple (`#3B1D54`) background, pure white typography, 0px border radius, 0.8125rem uppercase label with `0.1em` tracking. Height 48px, horizontal padding `space-lg`. Hover transitions smoothly to Basalt Slate (`#1A1A1A`) with a champagne gold hairline base.
- **Secondary Architectural:** Transparent background, 1px border in `#C5A059`, text in `#C5A059`. On hover, fills with `#C5A059` and text transitions to `#FFFFFF`.
- **Text Link / Action:** Crisp sans-serif label followed by a bespoke hairline arrow (`→`). Underlined with a 1px Champagne Gold rule offset by 4px.

### Cards & Property Modules
- **Editorial Cards:** Flat white or ivory background encased in a 1px `#E6E3DD` hairline. No drop shadows. Imagery dominates with a fixed 4:3 or 16:9 ratio, bordered cleanly.
- **Content Padding:** Ample inner padding (`space-md` to `space-lg`), separating architectural specs from descriptive narratives.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Input Style:** Minimalist bottom-line or full hairline box in `#E6E3DD`. Background is `#FFFFFF` or pure transparent.
- **Active / Focus:** The hairline crisp border shifts to `#C5A059` with zero outline ring. Floating labels transition in `label-caps` typography.

### Chips & Specification Tags
- **Appearance:** Rectangular (0px radius), outlined with a 1px `#E6E3DD` or `#C5A059` stroke.
- **Typography:** `label-caps` in Basalt Slate (`#1A1A1A`). Never bubbly; functions like architectural blueprint callouts (e.g., `4 BHK SKY VILLA`, `EAST FACING`, `6,200 SQ.FT.`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Geometry:** Square checkboxes (0px radius) and precision diamond or square-nested radio selectors.
- **State Colors:** Hairline stroke in `#1A1A1A`, checking fills with `#3B1D54` displaying a crisp geometric tick.

### Lists & Blueprint Key Data
- **Key-Value Layout:** Paired rows separated by 1px horizontal Travertine dividers (`#E6E3DD`). Property attributes (Ceiling Height, Balcony Depth, Unit Vastu) align with keys on the left in `label-caps` and values on the right in high-contrast `body-md` bold.
